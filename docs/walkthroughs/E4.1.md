### The `illustrative_only` question, resolved

E4.0 found nothing read it. Under Decision 1 it is the DECLARED FACT one
watermark reason derives from — a real job, not a redundancy. **The flag and
the origin answer different questions** — "is this value a stand-in?" vs
"how did it come to exist?" — so they may differ in ONE direction (a
LITERATURE value Isaac still flags illustrative is a conservative
declaration, allowed) and CONTRADICT in the other: `illustrative_only:
false` on a file whose origin is less real than LITERATURE claims a
defensible number with no defensible source. The loader refuses that
direction by name (C8.1's admissibility rule, one contract over), so the two
cannot be two sources of truth for one fact. Nothing copies the boolean into
a result; the verdict's `cause` cites it.

## §2 — Commit 2: the computation

`economics/cutoff.py: CutoffEconomicModel` — Contract 4's rule as
specified, per cell, per estimator, per confidence level: `minable iff
predictable AND (mu − z·sd) ≥ cutoff AND slope ≤ max_slope AND not
excluded`. Every estimator's surface, never cherry-picked; masked cells
never minable and counted, never dropped; `n_failing_cutoff` and
`n_failing_slope` each over predictable cells, stated as overlapping.

**What it refuses by name rather than computing quietly:** `grade_metric:
dollar_value` (every price is 0 and the corpus has ZERO GRADE rows — a
footprint that could not be computed is not a footprint of zero cells); a
NON-EMPTY exclusion set (rasterisation is not built; the file's emptiness
is asserted so its first polygon is a visible refusal); a stack with no
`slope` layer; cell areas that do not describe the grid; a null required
parameter (the loader, before the model runs); a level set without z = 0.

**The slope filter, applied and declared meaningless.** `max_slope_degrees:
6` is a collector limit; the stack's `slope` is Horn's gradient on ~11 km
cells, whose maximum on this grid is **0.87°** (median 0.31°). The filter
is applied as specified — not skipped, not pretended to mean something —
and `SLOPE_RESOLUTION_NOTE` travels beside the count. BACKLOG §3,
Checkpoint 1.

**The footprint is the WHOLE predictable domain — 347,707 km², 2,880 cells
of ~120 km² — under both scenarios, at the mean and one sd below it, for
all three estimators, and the difference map is empty.** This is a property
of the CUTOFFS' RELATION TO THE TRAINING MEAN (19.5 kg/m²; the corpus spans
11.6–26.8), not of the seafloor: kriging is within 0.5 kg/m² of the mean
over 99.62% of the domain and RF's plateaus lie in [15.1, 21.7], and at
z = 1 the far-field sd does not bring either below 10. The two scenarios
therefore bracket NOTHING on these surfaces — a finding about Contract 4's
PLACEHOLDERS, entered for G4.1 (BACKLOG §1), not a defect here. The test
asserts the measured sameness WITH its reason so a change to the
placeholders becomes visible.
