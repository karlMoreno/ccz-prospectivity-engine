### The `illustrative_only` question, resolved

E4.0 found nothing read it. Under Decision 1 it is the DECLARED FACT one
watermark reason derives from — a real job, not a redundancy. **The flag and
the origin answer different questions** — "is this value a stand-in?" vs
"how did it come to exist?" — so they may differ in ONE direction (a
LITERATURE value Isaac still flags illustrative is a conservative
declaration, allowed) and CONTRADICT in the other: `illustrative_only:
false` on a file whose origin is less real than LITERATURE claims a
defensible number with no defensible source. The loader refuses that
direction by name (C8.1's admissibility rule, one contract over), so the two
cannot be two sources of truth for one fact. Nothing copies the boolean into
a result; the verdict's `cause` cites it.

### The verdict (`economics/watermark.py`)

Two reasons, each DERIVED from a declared fact: `terrain` (the stack's
`dem_data_origin`; lifted iff MEASURED; lifts at Checkpoint 1) and
`economic_parameters` (the scenario's flag; lifted iff false; lifts at
Checkpoint 4). An undeclared DEM origin is an UNLIFTED reason. **The
discrimination property is tested**: a Checkpoint-1 world and a
Checkpoint-4 world each yield exactly one lifted reason — different ones —
where the lattice would have said AUTHORED for both. The lattice argument
is recorded in the module docstring so nobody "simplifies" it away.
